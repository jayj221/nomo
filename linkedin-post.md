## Alternate opening lines

The first two lines are the only ones people see before "see more", so they
carry the whole post. Swap in whichever sounds most like you:

- *"I deleted every dating app I had. Then I spent four months building one."*
- *"Dating apps don't have a people problem. They have a design problem."*
- *"The best conversation I've had this year started with a voice note from

## Posting notes

- **Put the link in the first comment, not the post.** LinkedIn suppresses
  reach on posts with outbound links. Say "link in the comments" or just let
  the video carry it, then drop nomosingle.com as your own first comment
  immediately after posting.
- **Native video only.** Upload the file directly. A YouTube link will get
  buried.
- **Add captions** to the video. Most of LinkedIn watches on mute, and your
  voiceover is doing the explaining.
- **Post Tuesday to Thursday, 9 to 11am IST.** Then stay on it for the first
  hour and reply to every comment, that window decides the reach.
- **The last line is doing real work.** Asking for criticism gets far better
  comments than asking for support, and comments are what actually spread it.
- **Don't post from a company page.** You have no followers there. This is a
  personal post about something you made.

---

## One thing to decide before you post

The post says the app isn't live. That's true and I'd keep it, because someone
will click through, find a waitlist form, and feel misled otherwise. Being
upfront costs you nothing and it's the difference between "founder shipping in
public" and "founder overselling a landing page."
