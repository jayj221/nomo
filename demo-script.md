# Nomo demo video script

A screen recording of the interactive demo at **nomosingle.com**, roughly 85
seconds. Record on desktop (the card is wider and reads better on video than
the phone layout).

**Before you start**

- Open nomosingle.com and scroll so the demo card fills the frame
- Click **Start over** so it begins clean
- Use the name **Jayant** and age **22** if you want the Naisha easter egg to
  land, otherwise pick any name
- Navigation is button-only now, so every move is a click on **Go further**

Timestamps are cumulative. Where a beat says *wait*, let the animation finish
before clicking, or the recording will look rushed.

---

### 0:00 – 0:06 · Opening

Static on the card at step one. Nothing typed yet.

> Nomo gives you ten matches a day. You don't see anyone's face until you've
> actually talked to them.

---

### 0:06 – 0:14 · Name and age

Type **Jayant**, then **22**. Type at a normal pace, don't rush it. Click **Go further**.

> It starts with almost nothing. A name and an age.

---

### 0:14 – 0:22 · Your profile

The three prompt cards stagger in. **Wait about 1 second** for all three plus the
hobby chips to land before speaking.

> Your whole profile is three answers, a few things you're into, and your music.
> No bio to agonise over.

---

### 0:22 – 0:28 · Who you are

Click the two options, then **Go further**.

> You say who you are and who you want to meet.

---

### 0:28 – 0:35 · Photos go in

The three photos rise in and the **LOCKED** pill drops. **Wait for the pill.**

> Your photos go in and they stay locked. Not even your ten can see them yet.

---

### 0:35 – 0:48 · Your number one

Click **Go further** to reach the sealed box. Let it breathe for a beat, then
click **Open it**.

**Wait the full 1.2 seconds** while it rattles and bursts. Do not click through
this, it's the best moment in the demo.

> Every day one person is picked as your number one. You don't get to skip
> ahead. You open it.

---

### 0:48 – 0:56 · Your ten

The ten names stagger in. **Wait about 0.8 seconds** for the list to finish.

> Then the rest arrive. Ten people, ranked. That's the whole day's supply.

---

### 0:56 – 1:03 · A profile

> You get their answers, the things they care about, what they're like at 2am.
> Everything except a face.

---

### 1:03 – 1:12 · The window

Click through to the phone notification. **Let the notification slide in and
pulse once** before speaking.

> At a time nobody knows in advance, a window opens. One hour. Call, or text,
> whatever you both want.

---

### 1:12 – 1:26 · Both of you ask

Click through to the chat. Click **Yes**.

**Wait the full 2 seconds** on "still deciding" with the typing dots. That pause
is the point, don't cut it.

> Either of you can ask to see the other. It only happens if you both say yes.

---

### 1:26 – 1:36 · The reveal

Click **Open it**. **Wait for the rattle and the reveal.**

> And then, finally, you see each other.

---

### 1:36 – 1:45 · Close

Click through **Still Naisha**, land on the match screen.

> Ten a day. One hour. No faces until you've earned them.
> Nomo. Waitlist is open at nomosingle.com.

---

## Notes

- **Total runtime** lands near 1:45 if you honour the waits. Cutting the three
  animation pauses gets you to about 1:15 but the reveals lose their weight.
- **The three moments that sell it** are the number-one unwrap, the window
  notification landing on a phone, and the mutual reveal. If you need a 30
  second cut, use only those three and drop everything else.
- **Record at 1440p or better** so the mono type stays legible after compression.
- **No cursor jitter.** Move deliberately between clicks; the demo has no
  hover states so there's nothing to show off with the pointer.
- If you're recording vertical for Instagram or a Story, use the phone layout
  instead and note that the dots strip and stage label sit tighter there.
