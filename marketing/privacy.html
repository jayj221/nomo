Privacy Policy

Last updated · 25 July 2026

Plain-English version. Nomo isn't live yet. Right now this site collects one thing from you: an email address, so we can tell you when your first window opens. This page tells you exactly what we do with it. A more detailed policy, reviewed by a lawyer, replaces this one before any real user signs up for the app.

What we collect right now

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and, if your browser sent it, the referring URL (so we know whether you found us through LinkedIn, a friend, or elsewhere). That's it. No name, no photo, no location, no tracking pixel, no analytics.

Why we collect it

  • To email you when your first window opens.
  • To understand where our early sign-ups come from, so we can talk to the right people.

Where it lives

Emails are stored in a database run by Supabase, on servers in Asia-South. It is not shared with anyone. It is not sold. It is not passed to advertisers.

How long we keep it

Until launch, plus a reasonable window after so we can reach the people who signed up early. If Nomo never launches, we delete the entire list.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your email at any time, no reason needed. Just write to jjoshi221@gmail.com and it'll be gone within a few days. If you're in the EU (GDPR) or India (DPDP Act 2023), you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data — the same email works for all of it.

Cookies and tracking

None. No cookies are set by this site, and no third-party trackers are loaded.

Later, when the app is live

The real Nomo app will collect more: photos, prompt answers, voice during calls, and the results of matching. All of that will be governed by a much more detailed policy, written with a lawyer, before you're ever asked to hand any of it over. When we get there, this page will link to the new one.

Contact

Everything data-related: jjoshi221@gmail.com. A human replies.
