How a connection happens

01

You're placed, privately

Upload your photos and our AI places you in a bracket. You see your placement exactly once — nobody else ever does, and neither do you again. You only ever meet people in your own bracket.

02

Ten people a day

Every morning: ten people, ranked one to ten by how much your vibe overlaps — ideas, mentality, music. No photos, no names. Just their three honest answers. Read them all. Mark who you'd talk to.

03

One window. One hour.

At a random moment each day, everyone's phone lights up at once. The window is open for one hour. Mutual picks become live voice calls — start with your number one, and if the vibe isn't there, move down the list.

04

Two reveals a day

When a call feels like something, ask for the reveal. If they ask too, photos appear — for both of you at once. You get two reveals a day. Spend them like they matter, because they do.

05

Names, then everything

Names come after faces. Then chat and socials — shared only when you both choose to keep going.
