# Nomo avatar prompts

40 prompts, one per roster slot. The filename is what matters: save each
image as that exact name in `nomo/marketing/img/`.

- `av1` to `av20` are the **women**
- `av21` to `av40` are the **men**

Square crop, 400x400 is plenty. Names are only for your reference, do not
put them in the prompt.

---
