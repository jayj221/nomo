# Nomo voiceover, ElevenLabs cut sheet

**Generate each chunk separately. Do not paste this whole file in.**

ElevenLabs has no concept of timecode. Anything in brackets gets read aloud or
wrecks the pacing. So the timestamps live here, not in the text box: you
generate 13 short clips, then drop each one on the timeline at the time given.
That's what stops drift, and it means one bad take only costs you one clip.

Placement times below assume a natural click-through of roughly 1:50. If your
recording runs faster or slower, shift everything after the first mismatch by
the same offset. The three animation gaps are fixed by the site's own code and
will not move.

**Voice settings:** Stability 45, Similarity 80, Style 15, Speed 0.95.
Low style on purpose. The script is dry, and pushing expressiveness makes it
sound like an advert, which fights the product.

## Notes

- **"nomosingle dot com" is written that way deliberately.** Type the URL and
  it reads the punctuation.
- **Generate chunk 16 first.** It's the line people act on, so if the voice
  can't land it, switch voices before you make the other fifteen.
- **The three silences are the product.** Every instinct says fill them.
  Watching someone wait is the mechanic, and it's the part a competitor can't
  screenshot.
- **Check chunk 07 and 12 for overrun.** They're the longest at roughly 8
  seconds each. If your click-through is quicker than mine, trim "Not a feed.
  Not infinite scroll." and "and you've either used it or you haven't" first.
- **Captions are mandatory.** LinkedIn autoplays muted. Chunk 01 especially has
  to be legible on the first frame or the hook does nothing.
