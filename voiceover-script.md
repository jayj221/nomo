# Nomo demo voiceover

Written to be read aloud. Roughly 180 words of speech across a 1:45 video,
which leaves deliberate silence around the three reveals. Don't fill those
gaps, they're doing work.

**Delivery:** calm, unhurried, slightly dry. This is not an ad read. The
product's whole argument is restraint, so selling hard undercuts it. Land
the full stops. Let the animations breathe.

---

## The script

**0:00** · *Demo card sitting still*

> Every dating app works the same way.
> A wall of faces. You judge, you get judged,
> and you swipe until none of it means anything.

**0:12** · *Typing the name and age*

> Nomo starts somewhere else.
> A name. An age. Three honest answers.

**0:22** · *Profile slide, prompts landing*

> The music you actually listen to.
> A few things you genuinely care about.

**0:30** · *Photos rise in, the lock drops*

> Your photos go in. And they stay locked.

**0:38** · *The sealed box, then the unwrap*

> Every day, one person is picked as your number one.
> You don't get to skip ahead.
> You open it.

*(let the unwrap play, no words)*

**0:50** · *The ten arrive*

> Then the rest. Ten people. Ranked.
> That's the entire day. There is no more.

**1:00** · *A profile, face still hidden*

> You'll know how someone thinks at two in the morning
> long before you know what they look like.

**1:10** · *The phone, notification sliding in*

> Then, at an hour nobody knows in advance,
> a window opens. One hour.
> Call, or text. Whatever you both want.

**1:24** · *The chat, both asking*

> And if it's going well, either of you can ask to see the other.
> It only happens if you both say yes.

*(hold through "still deciding", no words)*

**1:36** · *The reveal*

> Ten a day. One window.
> No faces until you've earned them.

**1:44** · *Match screen*

> Nomo. The waitlist is open at nomosingle dot com.

## 30-second cut

If you need something short for a Story or a Reel, use only the unwrap, the
window, and the reveal:

> Ten matches a day. No photos.
> You find out how someone thinks before you find out what they look like.
> Then a window opens, once, for an hour. You talk.
> And if you both ask, you finally see each other.
> Nomo. Waitlist open at nomosingle dot com.

---

## Notes

- **Say "nomosingle dot com"**, not "dot com" written out, or a TTS engine will
  read the punctuation.
- **If you're recording your own voice**, do three takes and keep the third.
  The first is always stiff and the second overcorrects.
- **If you're using TTS**, avoid the overly warm American startup-narrator
  voices; they fight the tone. A neutral, slightly flat delivery suits this
  better.
- **Music**: optional, and if you use it keep it under the voice, sparse, no
  build. A drop or a swell will make the reveals feel like an advert instead
  of a product.
- The word "algorithm" is deliberately absent. The site never uses it either.
